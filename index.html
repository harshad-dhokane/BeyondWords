Audio Normalization & Preprocessing

                STAGE 02
                Acoustic Model
              

              
Emotion2Vec+ Base MLP Classifier

              

                Extracts 768-D utterance latent vectors capturing pure pitch harmonics and vocal tremor. Normalizes with frozen training scalers Z = (X - μ) / σ through custom PyTorch 3-layer MLP classifier head.
              

            

            

              Checkpoint: emotion2vec_final_model.pth (928 KB)

                Compliance

              Compliance: PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR

                DeepMind
              

              
Gemini Multimodal Discrepancy (ASDI)

              

                Cross-modal reasoning compares acoustic valence against verbal transcript. Computes Acoustic-Semantic Dissonance (ASDI), detecting masked sarcasm, burnout, and distress when words and vocal tone clash.

          Core Mathematical Formulations
        


        

          
          

            
01 // De-escalation Velocity (ADV)

            

              ADV = (D_t - D_0) / (t - 1)
            

            

              Measures the average change in caller distress score per conversational turn. Negative ADV confirms active de-escalation.
            

          


          
          

            
02 // Sarcasm & Dissonance (ASDI)

            

              Δ = |V_acoustic - V_semantic|
            

            

              Divergence between vocal valence (-1.0 to +1.0) and text polarity. Δ > 0.70 flags masked sarcasm and unvoiced churn risk.
            

          


          
          

            
03 // Escalation & Distress Index

            

              min(100, 1.0·P_ang + 0.85·P_fear + 0.8·P_dis + 0.75·P_sad)
            

            

              Weighted composite of acoustic negative affects. Scores >= 68 immediately trigger supervisor handover and CRM alerts.
            

          


          
          

            
04 // Frozen Scaler Normalization

            

              Z_i = (X_i - μ_train,i) / σ_train,i
            

            

              Applies frozen 768-D training-set means and standard deviations from emotion2vec_final_model.pth to preserve latent distribution.

Beyondwords: Architecture & Engineering Whitepaper

            
Enterprise Affective Voice Intelligence & Closed-Loop Acoustic AI • Google Cloud Run

          


          

            
1. Executive Summary & Problem-Solution Alignment

            

              Text-based Large Language Models are emotionally deaf. In voice interactions, up to 70% of affective intent is communicated through non-verbal acoustic prosody—pitch fluctuations, speaking rate, trembling, vocal energy, and hesitation pauses.
            

            

              When an agitated customer or a despondent employee says "Yeah, I'm totally fine," traditional text-only AI transcribes the words, rates them as positive, and delivers a cold, transactional response. In customer support, this emotional blindness leads to customer churn costing enterprises $75B annually.
            

          


          

            
2. Hybrid Multi-Layer Architecture Overview

            

              Beyondwords deploys a 5-layer pipeline:
            

            
    
              
  • Layer 1 (Audio Preprocessing): FFmpeg 16kHz Mono 16-bit PCM normalizer with zero persistence.
    • 
              
  • Layer 2 (Acoustic Prosody): Custom PyTorch Emotion2Vec+ Base MLP with frozen training scalers.
    • 
              
  • Layer 3 (Compliance): Automatic PII/PCI-DSS token masking with SHA-256 cryptographic audit trails.
    • 
              
  • Layer 4 (DeepMind Multimodal Reasoning): Vertex AI Gemini 3.5 Flash evaluating cross-modal dissonance (ASDI).
    • 
              
  • Layer 5 (Closed-Loop Voice Synthesis): Google Cloud Neural2-F TTS prosody modulation.
    • 
              
  • Layer 6 (Stateful Trajectory Engine): Acoustic De-escalation Velocity (ADV) and Spiral Risk detection.
    • 
              
  • Layer 7 (Enterprise Distribution): Open CORS REST API & Zendesk/Salesforce automated webhooks.
    •
